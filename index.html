About

I'm Aron Jake Radam (also known online as Haru), a Lead Full-Stack Software Engineer and College Instructor working across production software and game development. I started out in game playtesting and QA at GridLock-Games Studio, testing builds, reporting bugs, and working with developers and designers ahead of releases.

Right now, I'm the sole full-stack engineer at PicStar Software OPC in Las Piñas, Philippines, building PicStar with Flutter and Supabase—from the mobile UI and PostgreSQL backend to real-time Top and Board feeds, Shineouts, rankings, and city-based filtering.

Alongside industry work, I teach sophomore through senior students at the University of Caloocan City, covering game design theory, prototyping, OOP, math in games, AI, networking, and production pipelines while guiding their projects from initial mechanics to full production.

Outside of my main stack, I study game design theory and build personal projects with C# (.NET), Java (Spring Boot), and Docker. I also use AI coding tools in my daily workflow to prototype and debug faster, and I'm open to freelance work or new roles.
