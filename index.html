About

I'm Aron Jake Radam (also known online as ), a Game Developer focusing on gameplay systems, Unity tooling, and technical game design. I got my start in game playtesting and QA at GridLock-Games Studio, testing builds and reporting bugs while collaborating with developers and designers on gameplay feel, balance, and release readiness.

After that, I worked as the sole full-stack engineer at PicStar Software OPC in Las Piñas, Philippines, where I architected PicStar with Flutter and Supabase and shipped it to the Google Play Store. That work sharpened my discipline in live backends, performance, and shipping production software end to end.

Today, I build open-source Unity editor tools and runtime systems to speed up designer and QA workflows while teaching as a College Instructor under the BS EMC (Game Development) program at the University of Caloocan City. I guide sophomore through senior students across game design theory, prototyping, OOP, game math, AI, networking, and production pipelines.

I also keep studying game design theory and use AI coding tools in my daily workflow to prototype and debug faster. I'm open to game studio roles, freelance projects, and collaborations.

Why @haruchanz64?

Haru means "spring" in Japanese, -chan is a casual Japanese suffix, and Z64 comes from the Nintendo 64 games The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask (my very first Zelda game) and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. It started as a gaming username, and I've kept using it on GitHub and my socials.
