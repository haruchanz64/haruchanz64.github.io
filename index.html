About Me

I'm a Lead Full-Stack Software Engineer and College Instructor who bridges production software engineering with game development. My path started in game QA and playtesting at GridLock-Games Studio, where collaborating with developers and designers on bug reproduction, release testing, and build stability shaped how I build reliable systems today.

Currently, I serve as the sole technical lead at PicStar Software OPC, architecting and shipping a live location-based social platform with Flutter, Supabase, and PostgreSQL—from mobile UI to real-time feeds and media pipelines. Alongside industry work, I teach sophomore to senior students at the University of Caloocan City, mentoring teams on game design, OOP, networking, and production pipelines. Outside of my core stack, I'm actively exploring C# (.NET), Java (Spring Boot), and Docker through self-study and personal projects, and I leverage AI coding tools in my workflow to prototype, debug, and ship faster. I'm open to freelance work or new job opportunities.
